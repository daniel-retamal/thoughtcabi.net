Privacy

thoughtcabi.net. Last updated 2 September 2026.

thoughtcabi.net is a bookmark cabinet that runs entirely in your browser. There are no accounts, no sign up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. Nobody running this site can see your cabinet.

What is stored, and where

  • Your cabinet, meaning your shelves, folders, cards and tags, is kept in your own browser's localStorage on the device you saved it from. It is not sent anywhere unless you connect a destination yourself.
  • Your preferences, meaning the theme, the view, the language, are kept in the same place and never leave the browser at all.
  • Nothing is stored on our server about you. No account, no profile, no identifier, no log of what you save.

When you connect a destination

You may point the app at a place of your own so the cabinet lives there too: a folder on your computer, a GitHub repository, Google Drive or your own server. In every case the cabinet is written as a single JSON file to the place you chose, and to nowhere else. It never passes through our server.

If you connect Google Drive, the app asks for the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. That scope can only see and change files this app created or that you explicitly picked. It is used for one thing: reading and writing your own cabinet file. The app cannot see the rest of your Drive, does not ask to, and never reads, indexes or transmits any other file.

Credentials

A token for a destination you connected is kept in your browser so that syncing survives a reload. Disconnecting a destination deletes it from your browser and, where the provider offers a revocation endpoint, revokes it. You can also revoke access at any time in your Google or GitHub account settings.

Signing in to Google passes an authorization code through a small service on our server, which exchanges it for a token and hands it straight back to your browser. That service keeps nothing: no database, no log of the exchange, and it never receives your cabinet.

Link previews

When you save a link, the app fetches that page to read its title, description and picture. Some sites cannot be read directly from a browser, so for those the request is made by a small relay on our server. The relay therefore sees the address of the page being saved. It stores nothing beyond a short lived cache of the page it fetched, and it never sees your cabinet, your other cards or anything identifying you.

Deleting everything

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes the cabinet completely, because that is the only copy we ever had access to. Any copy in a destination you connected is yours to delete there.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing that would identify anyone, of any age.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions go to the project's issues.
